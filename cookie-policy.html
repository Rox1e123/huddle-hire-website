Cookie Policy

This page explains what cookies this website uses and why.

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website can store on your device to remember information, such as your preferences or how you've interacted with the site.

What this site currently uses

The Huddle Hire website does not currently set any analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. We don't use Google Analytics or any similar tool, so we don't automatically collect data about how you use the site.

The site may use your browser's built-in local storage for small, non-tracking conveniences (for example, remembering that you've already seen a notice). This never leaves your device and isn't shared with us or anyone else.

If this changes

If we add analytics, advertising or any other non-essential cookies in future, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent through a cookie banner before any such cookies are set, as required by law.

Controlling cookies

Most browsers let you see, manage and delete cookies through their settings. Because this site doesn't currently set any non-essential cookies, there's nothing you need to opt out of, but you're welcome to check your browser's cookie settings at any time.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if our use of cookies changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

Before this goes live: if any analytics, advertising, chat widget, or booking tool is added to the site later, this page (and a consent banner) will need updating first. Cookie consent rules require that non-essential cookies aren't set until a visitor agrees.
