Privacy Policy

This page explains how Huddle Hire collects, uses and protects personal data, including CVs and enquiry details submitted through this website.

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

Who we are

Huddle Hire Ltd [registered company name and number to confirm], a recruitment agency registered in England & Wales, is the controller of the personal data described on this page. You can contact us about privacy matters at hello@huddlehire.co.uk.

What we collect

We only collect personal data that you choose to give us, through the two forms on our Contact page:

  • Employers: name, company, email, phone number, the role(s) you're hiring for, and any message you include.
  • Candidates: name, email, phone number, current role, your CV, and any message you include. Your CV may contain further personal data, such as your work history, education, qualifications and referee details.

We do not currently use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, so we don't automatically collect browsing data about visitors. See our Cookie Policy for details.

How we use it, and our legal basis

  • Employer enquiries: to respond to your enquiry and discuss your hiring needs, on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating as a recruitment business and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract with you.
  • Candidate enquiries and CVs: to assess your suitability for roles, to introduce you to prospective employers, and to keep you informed of relevant opportunities, on the basis of your consent (given by submitting the form) and our legitimate interest in providing recruitment services. You can withdraw this consent at any time by emailing us.

Who we share it with

We share candidate details with prospective employer clients only where relevant to a specific opportunity, and we aim to let candidates know before doing so. We may also share personal data with service providers who support our business (for example, our email and website hosting providers), acting only on our instructions. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for third-party marketing.

International transfers

We don't currently transfer personal data outside the UK. If this changes, we'll update this policy and put appropriate safeguards in place.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and candidate data for as long as it's reasonably needed to provide our services, typically no longer than [24 months, to confirm] from your last contact with us, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or we're required to keep it for longer (for example, for legal or accounting reasons).

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; have inaccurate data corrected; ask us to delete it; restrict or object to our processing; receive a copy in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@huddlehire.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to keep personal data secure and to prevent unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

Children

This website is intended for business use and is not directed at, or knowingly used to collect data from, children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
