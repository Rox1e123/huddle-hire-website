Terms of Business

This page sets out the terms that apply when Huddle Hire provides recruitment services to clients and candidates.

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

Before this goes live: this draft follows standard UK recruitment agency practice. Please have it reviewed by a solicitor familiar with the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003 before relying on it or publishing it live.

1. These terms

These Terms of Business govern the provision of recruitment services by Huddle Hire Ltd [registered company name and number to confirm] ("Huddle Hire", "we", "us") to businesses seeking to hire ("the Client") and to individuals seeking work ("the Candidate"). By instructing us to search for candidates, or by accepting an introduction from us, the Client agrees to these terms.

2. Our service

Huddle Hire acts as an employment agency, introducing Candidates to Clients for permanent positions. We search for, screen and shortlist Candidates against the Client's requirements, and introduce suitable Candidates for the Client's consideration. We don't guarantee that any introduction will result in a hire.

3. Introductions

A Candidate is considered "introduced" by Huddle Hire if we provide the Client with their CV, details or an introduction by any means, or if the Client becomes aware of the Candidate through our marketing or search activity. Our fee becomes payable if the Client (or any associated company) employs, engages or otherwise contracts with an introduced Candidate within the period set out in our agreement with the Client, whether for the original role or a different one.

4. Fees

Our fees and payment terms are agreed directly and in writing with each Client before work begins, and aren't published on this website. VAT is added at the prevailing rate where applicable. If you'd like to discuss fees for a particular assignment, please get in touch.

5. Rebate

Any rebate or replacement guarantee that applies if an introduced Candidate leaves a role shortly after starting is set out in our written agreement with the Client for that assignment.

6. Client responsibilities

The Client agrees to give us an accurate brief of the role and its requirements, to treat all Candidate details as confidential, to conduct their own right-to-work and background checks before employing a Candidate, and to let us know promptly if a Candidate is engaged, declined, or if the role is withdrawn.

7. Candidates

We don't charge Candidates any fee for helping them find a permanent role. Candidates are responsible for ensuring the information and CV they provide us is accurate. By registering with us, Candidates consent to us sharing their details with prospective employer Clients for relevant opportunities, as set out in our Privacy Policy, and can withdraw that consent at any time.

8. Confidentiality and data protection

Each party agrees to keep confidential any non-public information received from the other in connection with our services. Personal data is handled in line with our Privacy Policy and applicable UK data protection law.

9. Liability

While we take care in sourcing and screening Candidates, we don't warrant the suitability, qualifications, or conduct of any Candidate we introduce, and the decision to hire remains the Client's own. To the extent permitted by law, our liability under these terms is limited to the fees paid by the Client for the relevant introduction. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that can't legally be excluded.

10. Termination

Either party may stop working with the other at any time by written notice. Ending the relationship doesn't affect fees already due for introductions made beforehand, or the rebate terms above.

11. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of England & Wales, and any disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England & Wales.

12. Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to hello@huddlehire.co.uk.
